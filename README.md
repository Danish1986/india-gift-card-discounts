## How the prices work

Unyfy buys gift cards from its gift-card distribution partner and resells them below face value. Prices are synced every morning and shown on each brand page with their price history. Pro is ₹9 for the first month for new members, then ₹99/month. Discounts change daily; the brand page is always the live price.
